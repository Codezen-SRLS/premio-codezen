# Call for Applications – Premio Codezen

**Premio Codezen**, promoted by **Codezen, an Altairith Capital Holding Company**

> **2026 edition: concluded.** The test was held on **3 June 2026** and the prize was awarded to **Federico Roia** at the ceremony on **26 June 2026**.

## Purpose of the prize

Premio Codezen was created on the initiative of **Christian Vari**, security researcher and founder of **Codezen SRL**, an Altairith Capital Holding Company.

Codezen is an elite cybersecurity company specializing in finance and Web3 technologies, operating in Europe, North America, and Asia. Codezen's mission is to protect the integrity of traditional financial systems and decentralized applications, providing in-depth security audits and tailored advisory services.

With over **250 audits** completed, more than **900 vulnerabilities** identified, and **8.5 billion US dollars** in assets protected, Codezen is a global reference point in cybersecurity for finance. These results demonstrate the company's commitment to ensuring reliability and protection for its clients.

Deeply committed to technological innovation and the protection of intellectual property, Codezen holds several active patents. These patents reflect the company's dedication to developing advanced solutions capable of redefining security and reliability standards in finance and machine learning.

As a former student of **ITIS S. Cannizzaro**, Christian knows firsthand the challenges of the local area and the difficulties that young people face in establishing themselves in an increasingly competitive world.

Through this prize, Codezen aims to:
- promote merit and recognize the commitment of excellent students
- offer concrete support to overcome economic and social barriers when pursuing university studies
- encourage local young talents to pursue ambitious goals, helping them to enter a global context

With this initiative, Christian Vari wishes to give back to the local area by creating an opportunity that can become a springboard for young people, promoting innovation, education, and personal growth.

---

## Prize amount

- **Amount**: **€ 3,000**
- **Disbursement method**: reimbursement of university expenses (tuition fees, enrolment fees)

---

## Eligibility requirements

The following students are eligible to participate:
- students in their final year at **Liceo G. Marconi** or **ITIS S. Cannizzaro** in Colleferro
- students who intend to enroll in a university degree course in the **2026/2027** academic year

---

## How to apply

Interested students had to apply by **9 April 2026**, by filling in the **online form**.

---

## Competition structure

### Information session

Students will attend an introductory session on school premises, during which Christian Vari will present the prize and its objectives.

- **Date**: **9 April 2026**
- **Location**: school premises

### Assessment test

The test will be held on school premises and will be taken on a computer, under the supervision of teachers and representatives of Codezen to ensure fairness.

- **Date**: **3 June 2026**
- **Format**: computer-based, in person

The test will consist of:
- **Logic**: exercises to assess reasoning and problem-solving skills
- **Mathematics**: questions on key concepts
- **Cryptography**: introductory questions on the topic

Sample materials and past tests: see [`materials/`](../materials/)

### Award ceremony

The award ceremony was held on **26 June 2026** at the **Sala Alta Formazione, Biblioteca di Colleferro**, with the participation of Christian Vari and representatives from the schools.

---

## Evaluation criteria

The test will be evaluated digitally, ensuring impartiality and rigor. The prize will be awarded to the student who obtains the highest overall score in the test.

In the event of a tie, the prize will be awarded to the candidate with the lower **ISEE** (Italian household income indicator).

---

## Conditions of use of the prize

- The prize will be disbursed as a reimbursement upon presentation of receipts for university expenses.
- The prize amount will be disbursed within **30 days** of the submission of valid receipts.
- In case of renunciation, the prize will be awarded to the next ranked candidate.

---

## Dissemination

It is the responsibility of the schools, namely **Liceo Marconi** and **ITIS Cannizzaro** in Colleferro, to ensure adequate dissemination of Premio Codezen among students and families. The goal is to make sure that all interested students are informed and have the opportunity to participate in the initiative.
