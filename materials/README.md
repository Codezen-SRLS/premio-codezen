# Materials

Past tests and supporting materials for students and teachers.

| Edition | Format | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 (held 3 June 2026) | 10 multiple-choice questions (A–E), 50 min | [test](2026/test.md) | [test](2026/test-it.md) |
| 2025 | 10 open-answer questions, 50 min | [test](2025/test.md) | [test](2025/test-it.md) |

## Conventions

- One folder per edition: `materials/<year>/`
- `test.md` is the English version, `test-it.md` the Italian one; both must stay identical in content, numbers and option order
- Answer keys and solutions are never published in this repository
