# FAQ – Premio Codezen

## Who can participate?
Students in their final year at Liceo G. Marconi or ITIS S. Cannizzaro in Colleferro who intend to enroll in a university degree course in the 2026/2027 academic year.

## How much is the prize worth?
The prize amount is **€ 3,000**.

## How is it disbursed?
As a **reimbursement** of university expenses (tuition fees, enrolment fees), upon presentation of valid receipts.

## How do I apply?
By filling in the online form by **9 April 2026** (2026 edition, now closed).  

## What does the test consist of?
A computer-based test with questions on logic, mathematics, and introductory cryptography.

## What happens if two candidates get the same score?
The prize goes to the candidate with the lower **ISEE** (Italian household income indicator).

## Where can I find examples or past tests?
In the [`materials/`](../materials/) folder.  
A section dedicated to "previous years' tests" is available.
