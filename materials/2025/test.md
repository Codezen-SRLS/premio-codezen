## Questions

### 1 - False Truth

Eight scientists A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H participate in an experiment and make the following statements. Each one either lies or tells the truth, but no one is uncertain.

- **A**: "Exactly three of us are telling the truth."
- **B**: "D is lying."
- **C**: "G is telling the truth."
- **D**: "The number of true statements is even."
- **E**: "A or C is lying, but not both."
- **F**: "At least four statements are true."
- **G**: "B and E are telling the truth."
- **H**: "I am telling the truth if and only if F is lying."

**Which set of people is telling the truth?**

### 2 - Drone Inception

In a factory, drones are assembled. Each drone can be either model X or model Y.

We randomly select a line supervisor and ask two questions:
1. "Does the batch you control contain two drones?"
2. "Among these two drones, is there at least one model X tested on Wednesday?"

After a thorough search, we find a supervisor who answers "yes" to both questions.

**What is the probability that both drones in the batch are model X?**

### 3 - Packet Tracer

Ten hackers break into a secret network and download a huge archive of data packets, collecting them in a shared repository. After a day of work, they decide to go to sleep and split the packets into ten equal folders only the next morning.

That night, the first hacker wakes up curious and secretly goes to the repository. When he tries to divide the packets into 10 equal folders, he discovers that one is missing. He notices, however, that the sysadmin is illicitly holding one packet for unknown reasons, and tries to recover it: but as he approaches, the sysadmin discovers him and kills him.

Shortly after, a second hacker wakes up, finds his colleague's body (which has a positive side for him: now he can count on 1/9 of the original packets), and moves to the PC hosting the repository. He too, dividing into 9 folders, finds himself with one packet missing and tries to take the sysadmin's: but the sysadmin reacts the same way and kills him too.

The same script repeats, step by step, until the last hacker wakes up and, with no more obstacles, takes all the remaining packets.

**What is the minimum number of data packets that were originally in the repository, excluding those stolen by the sysadmin?**

### 4 - Portal Casino

In a simulated mental game, you find yourself in front of four encrypted portals. Only one contains a digital supercar; the others are garbage collectors that expel you from the system.

You initially choose portal 1. The system, which knows the contents of all portals, opens one of the other three and shows a garbage collector. Two portals remain closed, besides yours.

You are given the option to:
- Stay on your initial portal
- Switch to one of the other two remaining portals

**What maximizes your chances of winning the supercar? And what is the probability?**

### 5 - Robofinder

Consider a square grid with coordinates from (0, 0) to (6, 6). A robot starts at (0, 0) and must reach (6, 6) with moves only North or East.

The robot is required to stay above the main diagonal. Additionally, cell (3, 3) is blocked and cannot be visited.

**How many distinct paths of minimum length satisfy all conditions?**

### 6 - Friendly Fire

In a report, five statements numbered 1 to 5 appear:

1. "At least one of statements 2 to 5 is false."
2. "Exactly two of statements 2 to 5 are true."
3. "Statement 1 is false."
4. "Statements 1 and 2 are both true."
5. "Statements 3 and 4 are both false."

**Which subset of statements could be true without generating contradictions?**

### 7 - Hack the Lock

A device uses a four-digit code with all different digits (from 0 to 9).

After each failed attempt, the display shows two numbers:
- how many digits are correct and in the correct position
- how many digits are correct but in the wrong position

The following five attempts have been recorded:

| # | Code tried | Correct position | Correct digit, wrong position |
|---|------------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | 6942       | 0                | 2                             |
| 2 | 7106       | 0                | 2                             |
| 3 | 0125       | 1                | 1                             |
| 4 | 1749       | 1                | 2                             |
| 5 | 1092       | 1                | 1                             |

**What code unlocks the device?**

### 8 - Hacker Resilience

A collective of 100 hackers has been captured by a secret organization. Each of them has been labeled with a unique ID from 1 to 100. In a security room, there are 100 isolated virtual servers, each containing a different ID, from 1 to 100, stored in an encrypted file. The IDs have been randomly distributed among the servers.

One at a time, each hacker has limited access to the system: they can make at most 50 queries (i.e., they can open and inspect up to 50 virtual servers) to try to find their own ID. If they succeed in finding it, the access ends successfully; otherwise, the attempt fails. After each session, the system is reset, and changes are undone, so no hacker can know what was done by the previous ones.

The collective can agree on a shared strategy before the operation begins, but during the experiment, no communication is allowed.

If everyone manages to find their own ID within the 50 attempts, the hackers are freed. If even one fails, they are all arrested.

**What strategy is functional and optimal? And what is the collective's probability of success?**

### 9 - Quantum Table

Eight people sit around a round table to discuss important topics. Each person must greet another with a handshake.

However, there is a rule to follow: none of the imaginary lines connecting the hands can cross. It is said that crossing handshakes brings bad luck.

**How many different handshake patterns are possible?**
